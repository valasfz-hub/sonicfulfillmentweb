How it works

From Your Store to Your Customer's Door

Once your store is connected and your stock is on our shelves, every new order ships without you touching it.

  1. 01

    Connect your store

    Link your Shopify, Amazon, eBay, TikTok Shop or Whatnot store. New orders come to us automatically.

  2. 02

    Send your inventory

    Ship cartons or pallets to our Southern California warehouse. We count and check every unit in.

  3. 03

    We pick, pack and ship

    Each order is packed with care and labeled for UPS, USPS, FedEx or DHL, based on size, speed and cost.

  4. 04

    Customers get tracking

    Tracking numbers sync back to your store, and you see stock levels and order status as they change.
