Built for Days When Orders Triple

TikTok Shop demand does not arrive evenly. A creator posts, a video catches, or a LIVE runs long, and a normal week of orders lands in one afternoon. Shipping them late puts your shop rating at risk.

Sonic Fulfillment is a third-party logistics (3PL) warehouse in Santa Ana, California. We keep your inventory on our shelves and pick, pack and ship your TikTok Shop orders, on quiet days and on the busy ones.

You get back the hours you spend packing, and a partner who tells you first if something needs your attention.

Common questions

TikTok Shop Fulfillment Questions

What sellers ask before moving their fulfillment to us.

Can I use a 3PL instead of Fulfilled by TikTok?

Yes. TikTok Shop lets sellers ship orders themselves or through a third-party logistics provider. With a 3PL your inventory is not tied to one channel, so the same stock can also ship your Shopify, Amazon, eBay or Whatnot orders.

What happens when a video goes viral?

Tell us as early as you can when you expect a spike, such as a planned creator post or a LIVE. We plan labor and packing materials around it, and tell you right away if stock is running low.

How do my TikTok Shop orders reach you?

It depends on how your shop is set up. Tell us where you sell and what tools you use, and we will confirm how orders reach us before you send any inventory.

How is pricing set?

You get a quote built around your products and order volume. It lists the rate for receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging and shipping, and your invoice uses the same lines.

Do you handle Whatnot and other live selling too?

Yes. See Whatnot fulfillment, or all services for Amazon FBA prep, Shopify and eBay.

Where are you located?

Our warehouse is at 3731 W Warner Ave in Santa Ana, in Orange County, close to Los Angeles and the ports of Long Beach and Los Angeles.
