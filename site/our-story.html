Our story

A Southern California 3PL founded on a career in supply chain engineering.

Where we come from

Built by a Supply Chain Engineer

Sonic Fulfillment was started by an industrial engineer. Before founding the company, our founder led a large industrial engineering team in supply chain, working with some of the biggest brands in the United States and overseas.

That career was spent on the questions every seller cares about: how to move a product from the dock to the customer faster, with fewer mistakes, at a cost that makes sense.

Sonic Fulfillment brings that experience in supply chain, fulfillment and 3PL operations to online sellers of every size. You get a warehouse run the way large brands run theirs, with a team small enough to know your account.

How that shapes our work
