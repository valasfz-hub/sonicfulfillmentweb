Pricing questions

How Your Quote Works

Rates depend on what you sell and how much you ship, so every client gets their own quote.

How are my rates set?

We build your quote around your products and your order volume. It lists the rate for receiving, storage, pick and pack, packaging and shipping.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Your quote lists every fee before you sign, and your monthly invoice uses the same lines. Nothing gets added later.

What do you need from me to prepare a quote?

Roughly how many orders you ship each month and how many SKUs (distinct items) you carry. Send those through the quote form or by email.

How is shipping charged?

You pay the carrier postage for each package. We pick the service for each one based on size, speed and cost.
